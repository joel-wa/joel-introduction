01 / HEADWINDAI MEETS PLAY

A head-controlled flying game

Move your head.
Guide the bird.

Your webcam becomes the controller. A trained AI model reads your head position and guides a little bird between the pipes.

Play Headwind ↗ View code

    Look upThe bird rises
  • →
    Look straightThe bird stays level

    Look downThe bird descends
How to play & how the AI works

Try it in three steps

  1. Choose Play Headwind to open the game.
  2. Choose Enable Camera + AI and allow camera access.
  3. Click the game or press Space to start. Move your head to fly.

You can also play without a camera: W or ↑ moves up, S or ↓ moves down.

A trained model, a playful result

Headwind uses a trained Teachable Machine pose model to recognize three head positions: up, straight, and down. The game turns each prediction into the bird's movement.

Camera images are processed in your browser. The camera starts only when you choose to enable it.
